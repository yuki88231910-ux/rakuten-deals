Some of the best things in Japan — J-beauty, limited snacks, Japanese whisky, K-pop & anime goods, designer stationery — are sold on Japanese sites like Rakuten that usually don't ship overseas. The good news: you can still get them, easily.

The trick: a proxy shopping service

A proxy service (such as Buyee) buys the item in Japan on your behalf, receives it at their Japanese warehouse, and forwards it to your country. You don't need a Japanese address, a Japanese card, or any Japanese at all — the site works in English.

4 simple steps

  1. Browse our daily deals. Find a Japanese product you want on the Japan Finds homepage.
  2. Click “🛒 Buy & ship worldwide”. This opens the same item on Buyee in English.
  3. Order & pay on Buyee. Buyee purchases the item from the Japanese store for you.
  4. Receive it at home. Buyee consolidates and ships it internationally to your address.

What does it cost?

You pay the item price, a small per-order support fee, and international shipping (you can compare carriers at checkout). Buyee also lets you combine multiple orders into one parcel to save on shipping.
