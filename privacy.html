Privacy Policy

Japan Finds — last updated June 2026

Japan Finds (“we”, “this site”) is a curated catalog of Japanese products with links to third-party shopping services. This policy explains how your information is handled when you visit this site.

1. Information we collect

This site is a static website. We do not require registration and do not directly collect names, email addresses, or payment information. Standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) may be recorded by our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) for security and operational purposes.

2. Affiliate links and cookies

This site contains affiliate links to third-party services, including Rakuten and Buyee. When you click a link or make a purchase, those third parties may set cookies and process data under their own privacy policies. We may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. Please review the privacy policies of:

  • Rakuten Affiliate
  • Buyee

3. Analytics

If aggregated, privacy-friendly analytics are used in the future to measure traffic, they will be limited to non-identifying statistics. We do not sell personal data.

4. Social platforms

Our deals are also published to social platforms such as Bluesky and Pinterest. Interactions there are governed by those platforms’ own privacy policies.

5. Your choices

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Doing so will not prevent you from browsing this site.

6. Contact

For any questions about this policy, please contact the site operator at the email associated with this project.
